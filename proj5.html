Stack
Cribl Stream Splunk SIEM Slack PagerDuty
Challenge

Fragmented security telemetry across siloed systems created detection gaps, slow investigations, and exploding SIEM costs. Needed a scalable architecture where Cribl optimized data at the pipeline layer and Splunk served as the authoritative SIEM — enabling high-fidelity detection without uncapped ingestion costs.

Security Domains & Risk Coverage
Data Silos & Ingestion Chaos
  • Unstructured logs impossible to correlate across identity, endpoint, and cloud
  • No ingestion volume control or data quality standards
  • Analysts hunting across disconnected dashboards with no unified view
Detection & Investigation Gaps
  • No centralized analytics for threat correlation or behavioral baselines
  • Slow root-cause analysis due to fragmented evidence across tools
  • Limited threat hunting capabilities without unified telemetry
SOC Operations & Compliance
  • No unified platform for SOC workflows, SLA tracking, or reporting
  • Compliance evidence scattered across multiple systems
  • Inconsistent escalation processes and on-call response gaps
Implementation
Cribl Telemetry Pipeline
  • Centralized ingestion with schema normalization (JSON / common fields)
  • Noise filtering, sampling, and field reduction for SIEM cost control
  • High-value events routed to Splunk; bulk logs tiered to cheap storage
Splunk Detection & Analytics
  • Cross-domain correlation rules across IAM, cloud, and endpoint threats
  • Behavioral baselines and structured threat hunting workflows
  • Fast timeline reconstruction for incident investigation
SOC Operations Platform
  • Executive dashboards for KPIs, compliance posture, and reporting
  • SLA tracking with automated breach-prevention alerts
  • Unified SOC system of record across all telemetry sources
Alerting & Response
  • Real-time Slack triage alerts enriched with full incident context
  • PagerDuty escalation for critical on-call response routing
  • Automated workflows from detection → investigation → resolution
